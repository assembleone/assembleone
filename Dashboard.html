How FittersIQ Works

The permanent owner and staff guide for running FittersIQ.

What the Dashboard is

The Dashboard is the business control centre behind FittersIQ. Customers use Studio and Mobile for their everyday work. The Dashboard is where the owner manages customers, accounts, licences, subscriptions, payments, support and business information.

How the system connects

Studio, Mobile and the Dashboard run at app.fittersiq.com. Firebase is used for sign in and customer data.

The Dashboard address is app.fittersiq.com/Dashboard.html. The website is fittersiq.com.

The four main parts

🌐
Website

Customers discover FittersIQ, choose a package and create their account.

📐
Studio

Jobs, drawings, panel information, cutting information and QR information are prepared before fitting.

📱
Mobile

The fitter opens the job on site, scans QR codes, identifies panels and follows the prepared fitting information.

🗂️
Dashboard

The owner manages the FittersIQ business, customers, licences, subscriptions, payments and support.

What the Dashboard manages

  • Customer accounts
  • Subscriptions and plans
  • Users, licences and device allowances
  • Payments and payment status
  • Contracts
  • Temporary customer support access
  • Reports and business performance
